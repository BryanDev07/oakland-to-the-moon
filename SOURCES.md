## How the game uses these concepts
- **Segments** (launch, space, ground, science) frame the briefing and the design review.
- **Trade studies**: every component changes mass, cost, power, heat and capability at once.
- **Margins**: Δv, launch mass, power, battery, thermal and budget margins are checked before launch.
- **Concept maturity**: the hangar → review → flight loop mirrors iterating a concept until it passes review.
